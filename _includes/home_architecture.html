Platform architecture

A consistent operational model from edge to enterprise

Nova Vector connects devices, PLCs, sensors, and existing systems through deployment-appropriate interfaces. Nova Edge and EdgeX Foundry components can collect, normalize, buffer, and route data close to the equipment.

At platform level, devices and assets are combined with real-time and historical telemetry, state machines, commands, alarms, and notifications. Operations teams can use the same data in dashboards and OEE workflows, while engineering teams integrate it with MES, ERP, CMMS, and other applications through messaging and APIs.
