Platform architecture

A consistent operational model from edge to enterprise

Native OPC UA, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, and SNMP adapters can connect compatible plant and building systems directly. Where local or disconnected operation is required, Nova Edge and EdgeX collect, normalize, buffer, and route data at the site.

The platform combines devices and assets with telemetry, state machines, commands, alarms, tasks, production plans, work orders, and notifications. The same context supports dashboards, OEE, digital twins, AI outputs, and integrations with ERP, MES, CMMS, and other applications.
