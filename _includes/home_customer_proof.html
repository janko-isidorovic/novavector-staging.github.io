Bring modern and legacy machines into one operational view

In a mixed-generation production environment, modern equipment connects directly to Nova Vector through Modbus TCP and OPC UA. Older machines are retrofitted with appropriate sensors, an HMI, and a PLC, giving them a practical path into the same platform.

Both equipment types can then contribute to live production monitoring, plan-versus-actual reporting, downtime classification, alarms, and maintenance workflows. Optional RFID identification can add operator and support-team context, helping measure machine utilization and the time between an alarm, support response, and resolution.

  • Extend the useful life of existing industrial equipment
  • Compare performance across mixed-generation production lines
  • Give operations and support teams a shared record of events and action
