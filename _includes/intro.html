The Nova Vector Digitalization Journey

Connect Your Infrastructure → Digitalize Operations → Optimize → AI Enable

Get a 360-degree view of every important operational ROI metric—from downtime and asset consumption to team productivity, overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), and more.

With an edge AI pipeline, responsive digital twin, edge fleet, and lightweight Manufacturing Execution System (MES), the platform empowers you to embark on a clear business digitalization journey while effectively avoiding massive, disruptive system changes. Each step of the journey stands on its own, with no "big bang" required, eliminating the high risk of deploying new software all at once.

01

Connect your infrastructure

Connect sites, machines, building systems, and sensors, modern or legacy, and centralize their data in one platform, without replacing equipment.

02

Digitalize operations

Replace outdated, time-consuming spreadsheets with automated, live data collection to monitor every single step of your production cycle with accurate, real-time tracking. 

03

Optimize

Find and remove losses with real-time OEE, stoppage analysis, actionable alerts, KPIs, and personalized dashboards.

04

AI Enable

Nova Vector’s digital twin contextualizes collected data, building a strong foundation for edge AI, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance.
