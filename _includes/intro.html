Nova Vector 4.0 Digitalization Journey

Connect Your Infrastructure → Digitalize Operations → Optimize → AI Enable

Get a 360-degree view of every important operational ROI metric—from downtime and asset consumption to team productivity, overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), and more.

Effective AI implementation within your existing infrastructure without downtime depends on several factors, including your current tech stack, implementation costs, and the amount of contextual data you have. 

Nova Vector considers all these factors and other essential metrics to transform your current technologies into an AI-ready business ecosystem for reaping real operational ROI. 

With an edge AI pipeline, responsive digital twin, edge fleet, and lightweight Manufacturing Execution System (MES), the platform empowers you to embark on a clear business digitalization journey while effectively avoiding massive, disruptive system changes. 

Each step of the digitalization journey is individual, with no "big bang" required, effectively eliminating the high risk of deploying new software all at once.

01

Connect your infrastructure

Connect all your sites, building systems, sensors, and devices, including HVAC controllers, PLCs, and other direct connectors, to collect and feed data into an omnichannel, centralized, easily manageable dashboard and automate mechanical and electrical processes without implementing or deploying additional software. 

02

Digitalize operations

Abandon outdated, time-consuming spreadsheets and adopt live data collection to track and monitor every step of your production in real time. 

03

Optimize

Transform all digitized data into an innovative, proactive business strategy using Nova Vector’s automation, actionable alerts, KPIs/OEE, and personalized dashboards.

04

AI Enable

Nova Vector’s digital twin contextualizes all collected data and builds a strong foundation to enable your operations for AI-ready scenarios, such as automated production and predictive anomaly detection.
