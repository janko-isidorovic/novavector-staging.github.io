Nova Vector Core Operational Capabilities

IoT and edge computing services for organizations that depend on equipment-generated data to fuel decisions

Nova Vector eliminates the need for costly hardware standardization or expensive gateway projects just to test its viability for your business. Connect your existing devices, including modern and legacy gear, and start getting results right away.

Full-Scale Industrial Device Connectivity

Connect virtually any device, modern or legacy, including smart building and plant systems, and collect data from controllers, gateways, machines, and sensors using the standards you already have: OPC UA, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, SNMP, MQTT, and HTTP. Older machines without a readable controller can be retrofitted with sensors that deliver the same data set as modern equipment, without major investment or production downtime. Remote sites and low-power field sensors connect through the LoRaWAN network server bundled with the platform, and edge nodes add further protocols through EdgeX Foundry, all feeding one unified operational view.

Live Plan Accomplishment & Real-Time Production Visibility

Nova Vector provides a live, unified view of your operations, from a single device to an entire production line. Track current status, historical events, and daily, weekly, and monthly production plans in real time to measure output against targets. Live downtime analysis shows stoppages and their reasons, while ready-made OEE, real-time, and Stoppage Pareto reports highlight the biggest losses, with availability, performance, and quality per shift, product, and line. Teams see the losses while the shift can still act on them.

Smart Alerts, Notifications & Task Assignment

Automated problem detection lets you spot problems early and get them to the right people. When a machine stops or a value leaves its limits, the platform opens a task for the maintenance or support team that owns the machine and notifies them by e-mail. Each task is tracked from the first alarm to resolution, on boards organized by status, category, and owner, and is updated automatically when the issue clears. Recurring inspections and checklists run from templates, with blockers and signatures for critical steps.

Maintenance & Workforce Performance

Get immediate visibility into how reliably your equipment runs and how fast breakdowns get fixed, with Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) and Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) per line and station, calculated from machine states within planned shifts. Ready-made reports give maintenance teams clear insight into every production line and machine: failures, fault time per station, open and overdue work, planned versus unplanned work, and team utilization against the shift calendar. With an RFID badge scan at the machine, technicians record when they arrive, so response and repair times are measured, not guessed. The flow can be customized to match your maintenance process.

AI-Ready by Design with Built-In Edge AI 

Armed with Nova Vector’s Digital Twin, the platform makes your operations ready to embrace the power of AI by normalizing and contextualizing your operational data. It creates a strong foundation for process digitalization, anomaly detection, and proactive maintenance. Edge AI takes it further: run your own AI models on real-time data, on edge nodes next to the machine, with no round trip to the cloud. Every model version is registered, checked, and approved before deployment, rolled out with a compatibility check on each node, and rolled back in one step. Results return as ordinary data for dashboards, alerts, and tasks, and can act locally on the node when the connection is down.
