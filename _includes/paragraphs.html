Nova Vector Core Operational Capabilities

IoT and edge computing services for organizations that depend on equipment-generated data to fuel decisions

Nova Vector eliminates the need for costly hardware standardization or expensive gateway projects just to test its viability for your business. Connect your existing devices, including modern and legacy gear, and start getting results right away.

Full-Scale Industrial Device Connectivity

Connect virtually any device, modern or legacy, including smart building and plant systems, directly to the Nova Vector platform and collect data from controllers, gateways, machines, and sensors using the standards you already have.  Nova Vector handles web/mobile connections, industrial machine protocols, and modern wireless standards, giving you a unified operational view on the platform itself; no gateway project is required. Remote sites and distributed assets connect through a bundled, pre-installed LoRaWAN network server within the platform, supporting most modern protocols such as OPC UA, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, and SNMP.

Live Plan Accomplishment & Real-Time Production Visibility

Nova Vector provides a live, unified view of your operations, from a single device to an entire production line. Track current status, historical events, and daily, weekly, and monthly production plans in real time to measure output against targets. Live downtime analysis shows stoppages and root causes, while out-of-the-box Grafana reports highlight the biggest losses and provide actionable insights tailored to each process. Nova Vector gives teams enough time to act before issues go out of control.

Smart Alerts, Notifications & Task Assignment

Automated problem detection allows you to spot problems early and assign them to the right people through their preferred channels. Whether your equipment acts erratically or your values have gone haywire, the platform automatically assigns each alert to the right maintenance or support team. Smart alerts turn problematic events into assignable and trackable tasks with notifications, so you can monitor each problem from the get-go to resolution from a unified dashboard. Nova Vector will notify you about each event, including issue clearing, allowing you to organize every task by status, category, and campaign.

Maintenance & Workforce Performance

Get immediate visibility into downtime and productivity metrics directly from the platform, with Mean Time to Repair (MTTR), Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), and RFID readouts, to effectively assess how reliably your equipment runs and how fast breakdowns get fixed. Turn that data into out-of-the-box reports to provide maintenance teams with clear, actionable insights into every production line and machine. Additionally, RFID readers give you a full overview of people operating each line and machine, including the overall efficiency of your support and maintenance staff.  Assess how fast they identify, respond to, and fix the everyday production and performance issues, including the time it takes to recover from stoppages.

AI-Ready by Design with Built-In Edge AI

Fleet-Scale Edge & Remote Site Management 

Effective running of remote operations across multiple sites greatly depends on a reliable internet connection. If there’s no internet, you won’t be able to keep your operations in check. Nova Vector solves this problem with Edge runtime and remote site management.  The platform monitors your remote sites, including the on-site equipment, giving you full control over all processes, despite an unreachable cloud or unreliable internet. You control your software locally via an operator console with secure remote-desktop access, so you can take timely action and activate automatic rollback without visiting your on-site machines physically. As of 2026, Nova Vector has expanded this capability to a fleet scale, allowing clients to update multiple remote sites without a truck roll. The platform supports air-gapped plants too, making it perfect for customers who wish to isolate their operational networks from the public internet.
