Discover and bind compatible OPC UA, BACnet, and SNMP devices, and use reusable Thing Profiles to describe metrics and protocol addressing.

Retrofit legacy machines with additional sensors, an HMI, and a PLC, then bring those signals into the same asset and telemetry model.

Give operations and engineering teams comparable visibility into the condition, operation, and performance of both modern and legacy equipment.

Model and automate industrial processes

Represent industrial processes as explicit states and guarded transitions.

Connect transitions to alarm status changes, notifications, assignable tasks, and persisted command requests.

Store the state change and command request together, while a separate service performs execution asynchronously.

Simulate versioned process definitions, replay historical behavior, or exercise complete systems through protocol-level simulators before hardware is available.

Apply analytics and AI to industrial data

Nova Vector normalizes operational data and preserves device, asset, process, and production context for analytics and model-development workflows.

Use the on-premises AI assistant to query supported platform resources while keeping operational data inside the customer's deployment.
