Production Operations (Lightweight MES)

One of the greatest strengths of Nova Vector is that it provides you with a wide range of built-in capabilities to help you run the full scale of production operations, from packaging to shipping. Instead of flooding you with raw data, Nova Vector helps you plan every production step, from shift and production to consumption. 

The built-in capabilities include:

  • Detailed product catalog – assistance with all product cataloging details, from codes and packages to everyday customer specifications.
  • Track KPIs and measure productivity and asset utilization (OEE) – a full overview of all targets and thresholds, including line speed validation.
  • Live plan attainment tracking – track your actual output on daily, weekly, and monthly levels with out-of-the-box reports adjusted to your specific process needs.
  • Configurable work order documents adapted for production planning – turn every work order with straight-from-the-machine-counter details (manual overrides, serial numbers, and scrap/rework reasons) into a customizable document during shift runs to plan your production according to your needs, from initial phase to automatic completion.
  • Product recipes, routings, and consumption – get ahead of your product needs, from energy consumption and machine time to labor and materials; plan multiple alternatives and product versions ahead of time, and let the platform calculate actual vs. standard consumption for you, including per-order variance, input lots usage, and more.
  • Shift planning board and execution tracking – view real line capacity to assign and schedule work across shifts and dedicated machines via a drag-and-drop user interface. Track each position and task across the production line, identify downtime reasons ahead of time, and use RFID-based staff check-ins to measure production.
  • Manage downtime to reduce stoppages – Nova Vector’s downtime management gives you a live overview of your biggest losses, including all the details such as stoppage causes, duration, and count per line or machine. Once the platform captures, defines, and categorizes a stoppage, it triggers the right response, reducing downtime significantly.
  • Print out work orders and summarize shift reports – summarize shift reports and transform them into professional PDF documents, with precise consumption and production sections, to boost your management reviews.
  • Managed shop-floor printer fleet – a centralized and fully managed printer fleet helps you handle barcode and label printing on an industrial scale while you manage everything from a personalized dashboard.

Maintenance & Workforce Performance

Get immediate visibility into downtime and productivity metrics directly from the platform, with Mean Time to Repair (MTTR), Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), and RFID readouts, to effectively assess how reliably your equipment runs and how fast breakdowns get fixed.
Turn that data into out-of-the-box reports to provide maintenance teams with clear, actionable insights into every production line and machine. Additionally, RFID readers give you a full overview of people operating each line and machine, including the overall efficiency of your support and maintenance staff. 
Assess how fast they identify, respond to, and fix the everyday production and performance issues, including the time it takes to recover from stoppages. Clients get the full downtime and maintenance picture, from the moment the breakdown happens right to the resolution, without requiring manual data setup, coding, or adjusting configurations.

  Digital Twin — Model, Simulate & Replay

The Nova Vector Digital Twin allows you to run a what-if simulation to simulate changes and issues before they actually happen. Instead of spending time and funds on system changes that may or may not work for you, you can run a simulation on Nova Vector hardware. Harvest historical data to assess how such a change may impact your daily workflow.
Create a digital model of your operations and explore how each process runs in real time to understand how work moves through your equipment at each phase of production. 
In case you still don’t have the required hardware to run a digital model, Nova Vector provides you with its own simulated suite that mimics the real equipment down to a single assembly or production line. Script different scenarios, simulate network faults, and run acceptance tests directly in the platform to see how Nova Vector can help you optimize your processes.
The Nova Vector simulator suite includes OPC UA servers, MQTT edge nodes, SNMP network gear, BACnet HVAC, Modbus meters, PLCs, operator training, and testing.

Fleet-Scale Edge & Remote Site Management 

Effective running of remote operations across multiple sites greatly depends on a reliable internet connection. If there’s no internet, you won’t be able to keep your operations in check. Nova Vector solves this problem with Edge runtime and remote site management. 
The platform monitors your remote sites, including the on-site equipment, giving you full control over all processes, despite an unreachable cloud or unreliable internet. You control your software locally via an operator console with secure remote-desktop access, so you can take timely action and activate automatic rollback without visiting your on-site machines physically.
As of 2026, Nova Vector has expanded this capability to a fleet scale, allowing clients to update multiple remote sites without a truck roll. The platform supports air-gapped plants too, making it perfect for customers who wish to isolate their operational networks from the public internet.
