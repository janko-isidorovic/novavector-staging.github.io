Operational benefits

Industrial IoT solutions for production, equipment, and people

Nova Vector combines equipment data with production, maintenance, people, and process context so teams can identify losses earlier, respond faster, and improve with evidence.

Industrial connectivity for modern and legacy equipment

Bring modern equipment directly into Nova Vector through standards such as OPC UA, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, SNMP, MQTT, and other supported protocols.
Retrofit viable legacy machines with sensors, an HMI, and a PLC so they can provide many of the same operating and performance insights as newer equipment.
Model both generations of equipment consistently, giving operations and engineering teams one view across mixed production environments.
Start with the assets that matter most, preserve past capital investment, and expand connectivity when the value is proven.

Real-time production monitoring, OEE, and downtime analytics

Compare actual production with shift, weekly, and monthly plans in real time instead of discovering missed targets after the reporting period closes.
See work-order output, scrap and rework, stoppage reasons, material consumption, and standard-versus-actual variance in operational context.
Use OEE, downtime, MTTR, and MTBF to identify where capacity is being lost and where improvement work will have the greatest effect.
Begin with proven report patterns and adapt them to the way your equipment, production process, and management reporting actually work.

Alarm management and maintenance response tracking

Detect threshold breaches, missing data, stoppages, and process-state changes automatically, then notify the people who need to respond.
Convert alarms into assigned support or maintenance tasks and track them through resolution so problems do not disappear between systems or shifts.
Add RFID where useful to identify which team operated a machine, measure support response time, and understand team utilization alongside equipment performance.
Model process states, automate repeatable responses, and use simulation or historical replay to test changes before applying them to live operations.

Industrial edge management for remote and offline sites

Collect and process equipment data locally through Nova Edge and EdgeX when sites have limited connectivity or need to continue operating independently.
Give on-site teams a local operator console while central teams monitor node health, workloads, logs, and connected equipment across distributed locations.
Reduce routine site visits with remote access and centrally managed, signed software deployments that include health checks and rollback.
Edge fleet onboarding and rollout are integrated beta capabilities available for controlled early-adopter deployments.

Industrial AI and edge AI built on contextualized data

Build a usable data foundation as you improve daily operations. Nova Vector preserves device, asset, process, and production context instead of leaving AI teams with disconnected time-series data.
Use an on-premises AI assistant to work with supported platform resources while operational data remains inside your deployment.
When a use case is ready, deploy approved and signed ONNX models to selected edge nodes and use inference results in the same dashboards, alerts, and process models.
Edge AI is an integrated beta capability. Model training remains in external data-science tooling while an in-platform training workspace remains on the roadmap.
