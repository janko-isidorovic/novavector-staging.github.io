What You Get with Nova Vector

Nova Vector brings connected operations, production insights, built-in automation, and AI-ready tools together to help users solve real-world challenges—from managing remote sites and tracking asset consumption to improving production, reducing downtime, and turning operational data into actionable insights.


Native Multi-Protocol Connectivity

Connect machines, controllers, meters, and sensors, new or old, through the standards they already use: OPC UA, Modbus, BACnet, MQTT, LoRaWAN, and more. Remote sites keep their data even when the connection drops.

More about connectivity

Built-In Lightweight MES

Plan shifts, count production straight from the machines, and see every stop and its reason as it happens. Plan versus actual, OEE, and downtime are on screen while the shift can still act.

More about the MES

Platform and Edge AI

Run AI models next to the machines on edge nodes, or on the platform server for the whole site. Anomaly rules learn what is normal for each reading, and the AI assistant answers questions about your data and reports.

More about industrial AI

Digital Twin Workflow Simulation

Try a change on a digital copy of the line before you touch the real one. Replay past days, compare the results, and test new rules without stopping production.

More about simulation

Deploy and Scale According to Your Needs

Run Nova Vector in the cloud, on your own servers, or both. Fully isolated networks are supported too, set up for each installation. Start with one line and add sites as you grow.

More about deployment

Enterprise-Level Security and Multi-Tenancy

People sign in with their company account and see only what their role allows. Each organization, plant, and site keeps its data apart. Open standards mean you are never locked in.
