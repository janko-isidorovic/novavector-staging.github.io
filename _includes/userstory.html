24/7 Remote monitoring of soil and air

Off-the-shelf LoRaWAN weather stations and soil probes report air, rain, moisture, temperature, EC, pH, and NPK from every field, and pumps, irrigation, and storage equipment join through their controllers. Nova Vector learns what is normal for each reading, raises the anomalies, turns them into tasks with an owner, and shows how quickly the team responds.
