Platform walkthrough

One workspace for equipment, operations, and response

Each capability can deliver value independently. Together they preserve the operational context needed to improve performance across machines, lines, facilities, and remote sites.

01 · Connect and contextualize

Bring modern and legacy equipment into one operational view

Connect equipment directly through native industrial adapters or through Nova Edge. Reusable profiles describe metrics, registers, commands, and relationships so every signal has a consistent operational meaning.

  • OPC UA, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, SNMP, MQTT, Sparkplug B, and other supported interfaces
  • Device, asset, site, area, line, machine, and subsystem relationships
  • Telemetry, health, alarms, metadata, and command context

02 · Digitalize and optimize

See production losses while teams can still respond

Combine live machine data with shifts, plans, products, work orders, scrap, rework, stoppage reasons, and consumption. Supervisors can compare actual performance with the current plan before the reporting period closes.

  • Production plan attainment, OEE, downtime, MTTR, and MTBF
  • Work-order output, quality, serials, and material variance
  • Equipment and workforce performance in the same operating context

03 · Respond and automate

Turn operational events into coordinated action

Use explicit process states and guarded transitions to interpret equipment conditions. Route alarms to the right people, create assigned work, send configured notifications, and persist command requests for asynchronous execution.

  • Threshold, no-data, stoppage, and process-state alarms
  • Task ownership and response tracking through resolution
  • Versioned state machines with simulation and historical replay

04 · Operate from edge to enterprise

Keep remote and restricted sites useful through limited connectivity

Collect and process selected data close to equipment, provide a local operator console, and connect site operations with the central platform. Central teams can monitor approved workloads, logs, node health, and equipment without relying on constant site access.

Edge fleet onboarding and rollout are controlled beta capabilities. Edge AI uses approved, signed ONNX models; model training remains in external data-science tools.

Start with one high-value use case

Prove operational value before expanding the scope

Choose one measurable problem—a production loss, disconnected asset, remote-service challenge, or manual workflow. Establish the required data and operational context, validate the result, and reuse the same platform foundation as the deployment grows.

    Define

    Agree on the operational problem, baseline, users, and measurable outcome.

    Expand
