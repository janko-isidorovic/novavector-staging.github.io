Smart Agriculture

Efficient agriculture depends on real-time monitoring of soil conditions. Nova Vector's platform offers the tools needed to gather precise data, optimize irrigation, and make better-informed decisions.

Get Knowledge of Soil Parameters

With the use of the Nova Vector platform and soil sensors, landowners can save significant time and resources by obtaining real-time data on soil temperature, humidity, pH, and NPK values. By using the platform's dashboard, they can easily access current soil values and monitor them without having to manually examine the soil for days. The sensors can be plugged into the soil at various depths as per the client's requirements, making it a highly customized solution. Using the Nova Vector platform, obtaining soil data has become a lot easier and quicker than before.

Optimize Fertilizer Usage while Maximizing the Growth

Nova Vector collects soil measurements such as temperature, moisture, pH, and NPK values and presents them in operational dashboards. Farmers can compare current and historical conditions when planning fertilizer application and monitoring nutrient delivery.

Connect to Weather Services and Optimize Irrigation

The Nova Vector platform integrates with weather services and combines real-time weather data with current soil conditions, enhancing irrigation efficiency for improved agricultural output. Combining weather information with soil conditions provides a stronger data foundation for precision farming practices.
