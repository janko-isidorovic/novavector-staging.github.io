Smart City

Nova Vector connects distributed infrastructure and sensors for water, waste, traffic, parking, and environmental monitoring.

Smart Water Management

Smart water deployments can combine non-intrusive surface-mounted sensors with operational analytics to identify flow changes and investigate possible leaks. Earlier detection helps utilities prioritize field work and reduce avoidable water loss without relying on unsupported industry-wide statistics.

Our solution for smart water management

  • Surface Mounted Sensors on the pipes that measure water flow (no need to change the pipes or make holes to mount sensors).
  • Analytics that based on gathered data locates leaking pipes early and can create maintenance tasks.
  • In later phases we can develop predictive AI/ML models which can predict the locations where the leakage can occur.

Smart Waste Management

Smart waste deployments use sensors to measure container fill levels and provide operational data for route planning and service coordination. The same platform can monitor sewage levels and connected manhole covers where suitable sensors and integrations are deployed.

Traffic Monitoring and Smart Parking

Traffic-monitoring and smart-parking solutions use connected cameras and sensors to measure people flow, traffic patterns, vehicle speeds, and parking occupancy. Numeric measurements are sent to the platform without transmitting images. Administrators can configure alarms, notify city services, and track tasks and resolution times.

City Vector

City Vector combines approved data sources into operational indicators for traffic, utilities, parking, air quality, and public-building utilization. APIs can provide controlled access to selected real-time and historical data for authorized applications and partners.
