Manufacturing Operations

Connect production equipment and operational systems to monitor production plans, OEE, stoppages, maintenance activities, and real-time process data.

Why Nova Vector Smart Industry Solutions:

  • Real-Time Insight into Industrial Operations
  • Seamless Integration with Existing Infrastructure
  • Enhanced Maintenance Strategies with Nova Vector
  • Real-Time Analytics for Production Optimization
  • Efficient Automation through API Integration

Production Planning Module

The Production Planning Module monitors per-shift production-plan execution for connected production lines. Plans can be entered manually or supplied through an approved ERP integration. Actual production, line speed, efficiency, and changeover data can then be compared with the plan. Optimization functionality is described only for deployments in which it has been validated.

OEE Dashboards

Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) combines availability, performance, and quality into a consistent view of production effectiveness. Nova Vector dashboards use production and equipment data to show which factors are affecting a line, shift, or product, helping operations teams investigate losses and bottlenecks.

Record and Classify Stoppages

Nova Vector records production-line stoppages from connected data sources and allows operators to classify their causes. Historical stoppage data can then be analyzed by line, product, shift, type, count, and duration to support maintenance and production-improvement work.

Get Insight into Stoppage Type, Count and Duration

With Nova Vector, manufacturers can leverage customizable reports to gain valuable insights into stoppage patterns, including types, counts, and durations. By filtering data according to production lines, products, and date/time, clients can detect trends, enhance planning, and construct strategies to minimize unexpected stoppages. This data-centric methodology promotes proactive decision-making, streamlines production efficiency, and boosts overall operational performance.

Realtime Data Visualization

Nova Vector's real-time data visualization equips managers with instant insights to make efficient decisions. Intuitive visuals make tracking factory operations effortless, allowing for proactive responses and enhanced operational efficiency.

Track Maintenance Crew Tasks and Results

Nova Vector streamlines maintenance crew task tracking and analysis with RFID readers and ID cards. The maintenance crew can easily record activities, enabling efficient monitoring and analysis of tasks and outcomes.

Realtime Plan Execution Tracking

Nova Vector's Real-time Plan Execution Tracking, using RFID card readers, makes maintenance crew task monitoring more manageable. A quick swipe records activities and provides real-time updates on task execution, increasing visibility, control, and maintenance efficiency.
