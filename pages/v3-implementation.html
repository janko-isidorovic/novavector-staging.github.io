Scripted scenarios such as a breakdown, a changeover, or a full shift run against a simulated line with expected results, so reports, alerts, and integrations are proven before the real equipment is switched over. image: /assets/img/v3/features/10-simulators/production-line-scenarios.png image_alt: Nova Vector Simulators console with scripted scenarios for a simulated production line caption: Scripted acceptance scenarios with expected results, run against a simulated production line. - eyebrow: Handover heading: A system your team can operate, and a team behind it paragraphs: - The deployment is documented, the responsible teams are trained by role, and the support path is agreed.
