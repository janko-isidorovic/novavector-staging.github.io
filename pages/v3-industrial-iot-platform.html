Equipment from many vendors and protocols becomes one device registry with live data, while single sign-on, roles, and separated sites keep access under control, on premises or in your cloud. duration: "4:33" faq: - question: Which deployment models are supported? answer: The standard platform deployment runs as Docker containers on one x86-64 Linux server, on premises or in a customer-controlled cloud. Hybrid deployments add managed edge nodes next to the equipment. Kubernetes and isolated-network deployments are prepared for each project rather than shipped as standard packages. - question: Which industrial protocols are available? answer: The platform connects over OPC UA, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, SNMP traps, MQTT, HTTP, and WebSocket, and over LoRaWAN for long-range, low-power sensors, which is in production use in smart agriculture. Edge nodes add more device protocols through EdgeX Foundry. CoAP is in pilot. Discovery, write, and command support differ per protocol. - question: What about older machines without a readable controller? answer: Older machines can be retrofitted with sensors that deliver the same data set as modern equipment, such as state, counts, and energy, without major investment or production downtime. - question: Can we start with one machine or one site? answer: Yes.
