Hybrid deployments add managed edge nodes. Kubernetes and isolated-network deployments are delivered as project-specific services rather than standard packaged installations. - question: Which industrial protocols are available? answer: Current platform connectivity includes MQTT, HTTP, WebSocket, OPC UA, Modbus TCP, BACnet/IP, and SNMP, with EdgeX available for site-side device services. CoAP and LoRaWAN are pilot capabilities. Exact discovery, security, write, and command behavior depends on the selected adapter and deployment. - question: Can we start with one machine or one site? answer: Yes.
