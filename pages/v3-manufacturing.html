A KPI links a product to a machine with its validated speed in pieces per minute, which the planning board uses to work out how much fits into a shift. image: /assets/img/v3/features/06-manufacturing-operations/products.png image_alt: Product definitions with product codes caption: Products with their codes, shared by the line's data and the ERP. - eyebrow: Work orders and planning heading: Turn demand into an executable shift plan paragraphs: - A work order carries the product, target quantity, dates, priority, and related production context. The shift planning board places approved work onto the right line and shift so supervisors have a practical operating plan for the day. - Validated product speeds and planned stoppages such as changeovers, cleaning, or breaks set the capacity the board works with. A supervisor approves the plan before it runs. image: /assets/img/v3/features/06-manufacturing-operations/shift-planning-board.png image_alt: Nova Vector manufacturing shift planning board caption: The planning board places work orders onto production lines and shifts before execution. - eyebrow: Production execution heading: Count production from the line, not from paper paragraphs: - For each approved plan linked to a work order, the platform adds up the machine's readings within the shift as good pieces, scrap, and rework. Figures update as readings arrive, and an edge node or decoder can turn a machine's running counter into such readings. - The work order shows produced, scrap, rework, and final quantities with progress against the order, and moves to its completed status on its own when every item is produced. Production records entered by people can add to or correct the counted figures. image: /assets/img/v3/features/06-manufacturing-operations/work-order-production.png image_alt: Nova Vector work-order production view caption: Work-order production connects current output with the order and production context responsible for it. - eyebrow: Real-time reports heading: See OEE and the biggest losses while the shift runs paragraphs: - The real-time line report shows production per minute by product, OEE against its target, and availability, performance, and quality. The Stoppage Pareto ranks stop time by reason, and the equipment reliability report shows MTBF and MTTR per station. - These reports come as ready-made templates and can be adapted per line. See OEE and downtime for the full set, including maintenance team reports. image: /assets/img/v3/screens/oee-realtime-report.png image_alt: Real-time line report with production per minute and OEE caption: The real-time line report, with output per minute, OEE against its target, and availability, performance, and quality. - eyebrow: Recipes and routings heading: Define what a product should consume and how it should be made paragraphs: - Versioned recipes describe required materials, while routings describe process steps and operations. Alternatives can be represented where the process allows substitution or more than one valid path. - During execution, actual material, labor, machine time, or energy use can be recorded against the work order. Standard-versus-actual comparisons then show quantity and cost variance for the specific production order. image: /assets/img/v3/features/06-manufacturing-operations/recipe.png image_alt: Nova Vector manufacturing recipe caption: Versioned recipes define the bill of material used as the standard for production and consumption comparison. - eyebrow: Consumption heading: Make material variance visible while the production record is still intact paragraphs: - Consumption records connect input lots and actual usage to the plan and work order that consumed them, so the expected recipe quantity can be compared with what was actually recorded on the shop floor. - Connections to scales, ERP, or other shop-floor systems are implemented for the customer's environment rather than assumed to be universal built-in connectors. Work orders, plans, and production figures are available through the platform's API. image: /assets/img/v3/features/06-manufacturing-operations/plan-consumption.png image_alt: Nova Vector manufacturing plan consumption caption: Consumption views compare expected and actual material use within the production plan. - eyebrow: Downtime, people, and attendance heading: Explain lost production with both machine state and human context paragraphs: - Reports derive stops from machine states, and people record the category and solution of stoppages on the plan. Each stoppage is also kept as a resolved task, so downtime, OEE, MTTR, and MTBF can be analyzed with the maintenance work behind them. - Each plan lists the people on the shift and the shift leader. Where badge readers are part of the project, RFID badges at the line record check-in and check-out against the plan, and a badge scan at the machine marks when a technician arrives; the flow can be customized to match the customer's maintenance process. image: /assets/img/v3/features/04-alerts-notifications-and-tasks/task-details.png image_alt: Nova Vector operational task details caption: Operational tasks keep the equipment and response context of a production issue. - eyebrow: Assembly lines heading: Track serialized assembly with live line data paragraphs: - The same building blocks apply to assembly lines with serialized products. A live line report can show units through each station, cycle times, rework, cart and station signals, and environmental conditions such as hall temperature and humidity. - Edge AI can add a model next to the line, for example one that flags conditions associated with electrostatic-discharge risk. Every model version is approved before it is deployed to the edge nodes, and its results arrive as ordinary data for reports, alerts, and tasks. image: /assets/img/v3/screens/assembly-line-live-report.png image_alt: Live assembly line report with units per minute, cycle time, battery levels, and hall conditions caption: A live assembly line report, with units per minute, cycle time, cart batteries, alarm codes, and hall temperature and humidity. - eyebrow: Labels and shop-floor systems heading: Print labels from the work order paragraphs: - Zebra label templates in ZPL fill in fields from the work order and the item, such as product, quantity, produced quantity, and order number. Printers are registered by network address, and the number of copies can be fixed, follow the item quantity, or come from the work order.
