A Pareto of fault time per station shows where repair time goes. - Failures per day and a failure timeline help separate a one-off breakdown from a pattern that needs a maintenance plan. image: /assets/img/v3/screens/equipment-reliability-report.png image_alt: Equipment reliability report with failures, MTBF, MTTR, availability, and fault time per station caption: Failures, MTBF, MTTR, and availability for the line, the same per station, and a Pareto of fault time per station. - eyebrow: Maintenance team heading: Measure response and repair, not just the machine paragraphs: - Team reports show open and overdue work per person, lead time and on-time completion, response to machine problems, planned versus unplanned work, and team utilization against each person's shift calendar. - Utilization uses the time people log on their tasks and, where nothing is logged, time estimated from the machine states. Where RFID readers are part of the project, a badge scan at the machine marks when the technician arrives, so response and repair times are measured rather than guessed; the flow can be customized to match the customer's maintenance process. image: /assets/img/v3/screens/maintenance-work-board.png image_alt: Maintenance work board with each technician's open work, load, and capacity caption: The maintenance work board, with each technician's open work, estimated load and capacity, and overdue and urgent tasks. - eyebrow: Plan attainment heading: See whether the shift is producing what was approved paragraphs: - Nova Vector connects the approved shift plan with the work orders running on each line.
