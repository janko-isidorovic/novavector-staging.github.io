Older machines without a readable controller are retrofitted with sensors that deliver the same data set as modern equipment, without major investment or production downtime. - Edge nodes at the line collect the data, keep local logic running, and are managed as one fleet from the platform. image: /assets/img/v3/screens/resources/machine-live-data.png image_alt: Nova Vector machine page with a live chart of the machine's values and its readings as they arrive caption: One page per machine, with every value it sends charted live. Customers that run Kubernetes, for example with Rancher, get a Kubernetes deployment as a project service, and isolated or air-gapped networks are prepared per installation. - High availability, backups, retention, and update windows are designed with the customer's IT for each project rather than assumed. - eyebrow: Enterprise systems heading: Built for your data model, not a generic connector paragraphs: - There are no one-size-fits-all ERP or MES connectors. Each integration is designed for the customer's data model, authentication, and direction of data flow, typically work orders and products in, and produced, scrapped, and consumed quantities, stoppages, and maintenance records out. - Integrations are built on the REST APIs, Node-RED flows, or custom functions, and external systems authenticate with their own service accounts and see only the records of their groups. image: /assets/img/v3/features/11-automation-and-integration/open-api.png image_alt: Nova Vector OpenAPI documentation caption: Integrations use the same documented APIs as the Nova Vector console. - eyebrow: Customize heading: Reports, processes, and workflows shaped to your plant paragraphs: - Ready-made OEE, stoppage, reliability, and team reports are adapted to your lines, shifts, and products. Process rules model how your machines depend on each other, and task workflows follow your maintenance and quality procedures, including RFID badge scans at the machine, customized to match your maintenance process flow. image: /assets/img/v3/screens/resources/maintenance-work-board.png image_alt: Nova Vector maintenance work board with each technician's open work, load, and capacity caption: A maintenance work board configured around the team and its capacity. Screenshot from a demo installation. - eyebrow: Test before go-live heading: Prove integrations against a simulated line paragraphs: - MES and ERP links are usually tested late, on the real line, and nobody can order a bad lot or a breakdown on demand. Nova Vector Simulators replace that guesswork with a simulated assembly or production line that speaks the same protocols as the real equipment. - The simulated line has a known ground truth. Scripted scenarios, such as a bad parts lot that sends servers to rework or a serial used twice, check every step with expected results, so the records your MES and ERP integrations depend on are verified before production depends on them. image: /assets/img/v3/features/10-simulators/assembly-line.png image_alt: Nova Vector Simulators assembly line with stations, badge readers, and test results caption: A simulated assembly line used to test integrations before go-live. - eyebrow: Handover and support heading: Deliver a system the responsible team can operate paragraphs: - Implementation includes configuration, integration, testing, documentation, role-based training, and an agreed support path.
